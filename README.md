The one line a talking elevator would say to its passengers:

> I have carried your secrets, your groceries and your ex — please stop pressing all the buttons like a piano.

The elevator speaks in its own voice about its own job — carrying people and their baggage, literally and emotionally — then turns on the passenger with the universal sin of button-mashing, likened to playing piano. Surprise twist, no pun, fresh angle.

Pure Python stdlib, no network, no secrets. `main.py` writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and
verifies it byte-for-byte against a self-check gate (1–120 characters,
single line, exactly one sentence).
